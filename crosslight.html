How to play

Six hidden five-letter words cross in a waffle: three across, three down. Tap a square to pick a word, then guess it. Tap the same square again to switch between across and down.

Green letters are in the right spot and stay on the grid, so they help the crossing words too. Yellow means the letter is in this word somewhere else. Blue means it isn't in this word but is in one of the other hidden words.

You share one pool of 15 guesses across the whole grid. Letters you've already revealed fill themselves in as you type.
