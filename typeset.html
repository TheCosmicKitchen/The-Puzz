How to play

Everyone gets the same sheet and the same seven pieces of type today. You get one play: lay some of your tiles in a single row or column so they join the words already on the sheet. Every word your tiles make, across and down, has to be a real word.

Tap a tile and then a square, or drag it there. On a keyboard, click a square and type; click it again to switch between across and down. Tap a placed tile to take it back. Try as many layouts as you like: the line under the sheet shows what each one scores. When you're happy, lock it in.

2LDoubles the letter on it
3LTriples the letter on it
2WDoubles the whole word
3WTriples the whole word

Bonus squares only count for tiles you place this turn. Use all seven tiles for a +35 full set bonus.

Today's record is the best play on the sheet using everyday words. You see it once you've locked in, and so does everyone else, so you can compare. Rarer words are allowed too, so it can be beaten.
