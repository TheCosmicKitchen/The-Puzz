LadderPrototype

Change one letter at a time to climb from the top word to the bottom one.
How to play

Each rung must be a real four-letter word that differs from the rung above by exactly one letter, in the same position. Reach the bottom word. Par is the shortest possible climb.

Hint shows the next rung on a shortest route from where you are, faintly. Type it to use it.
