Sign book
  • Drag the locomotive onto the board and drive it to the buffer stop at the end of the line. Track can't run over itself. Drag back to take track up, or tap anywhere on your track to jump back to that point. Arrow keys work too.
  • Stop. The train must call here, at a junction or along a stretch of track.
  • Buffers. The line can't pass this junction.
  • Broken track. No line along this stretch, unless you're carrying a spare rail.
  • Signal. The route runs along exactly as many sides of this square as the signal has lamps.
  • Stations. Your route cuts the map into areas. Each area may only hold stations of one company colour.
  • Platform board. The area holding this board is exactly that many squares.
  • Tickets. Every ticket must share its area with exactly one other ticket of the same colour.
  • Spare rails. Pass this junction to pick up a spare. Each spare repairs one broken stretch further down the line, so collect it first.
  • Tunnel portal. Pass it to pick up a tunnel. Later you can cross under your own track once, straight across a straight stretch, leaving a gap in your tail.
