Tap a number, an operation, then another number.
How to play

Combine the six numbers with + − × ÷ to make the target. Each number can be used once, and every result becomes a new number you can use again. You don't have to use them all. No fractions or negative numbers.

Every target can be hit exactly. Par is the fewest steps it can be done in.
