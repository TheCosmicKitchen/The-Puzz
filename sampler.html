How to play

The numbers beside each row and above each column are runs of stitches, in order. “3 1” means three stitches in a row, at least one bare square, then one more. Work out which squares get a stitch and the pattern appears.

Mark squares you know stay bare with a dot. Every pattern can be solved by logic alone, without guessing.
