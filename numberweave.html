How to play

Every line is a little sum, read left to right or top to bottom. Fill the empty squares with the tiles so every sum is true. Sums cross, so one number can belong to two of them.

^ is a power: 2 ^ 3 = 8. mod is the remainder after dividing: 17 mod 5 = 2.

Easy is a single square of four sums with + and −. Medium, Hard and Expert grow bigger crosswords and bring in ×, ÷, ^ and mod. Harder levels slip in a few tiles you won't need, and on Expert some operators are missing too. Any way of filling it that makes every sum true counts.
