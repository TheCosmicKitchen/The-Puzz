How to play
  • Fill every empty hex from the basket. The basket holds exactly the pieces you need, no more.
  • Each pattern hex asks how its six neighbours group. Every row of holes is one fabric, and each row is a different fabric. As you sew neighbours in, the holes fill with the fabrics you used, so you can see what is still missing. This one wants three of one fabric, two of another and one of a third.
  • Stitched pairs. Hexes outlined together take a two-fabric piece from the basket. Pieces never rotate: a pair with red on the left only fits a slot pointing the same way, red end first.
  • A crossed swatch means that fabric can't go in that hex.
  • A stitch on an edge links two hexes: = means same fabric, ≠ means different fabrics.
  • Pinned tiles are already sewn in and can't move. Tap a pattern hex to outline its neighbours.
