Cross out numbers until every row and column adds up to its target.
How to play

Each row and column has a target. Strike out numbers so the ones left add up to every target at once. A target turns green when its line adds up, and red when what's left is already too small to reach it.

Use Keep to circle numbers you're sure about. Circles are just notes; only struck numbers change the sums.
