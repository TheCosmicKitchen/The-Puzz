Add a lab

Any game can have a lab. Give it a folder with an index.json listing its reports (each with an id, a headline, and the version and date it ran on), then add it to lab/labs.json. It shows up here with its newest reports.

Dungeon Scrawl is the template: tools/scrawl_sim.py publish writes scrawl-lab/index.json after every run.
