Dungeon Scrawl

You
How to play

Beat today's three foes. Everyone gets the same foes and the same tiles.

Each turn you draw six letter tiles. Tap a tile, then a card's slot. Nudge moves a letter one step on (B→C, Z→A). Flip mirrors it across the alphabet (A↔Z, B↔Y, M↔N). Changed tiles come back to your hand, so you can keep working on them.

Spellblade takes a real word of three letters or more and deals its points, plus a bonus for long words. Quill throws one consonant. Vowel Ward turns a vowel into a shield for the foe's next move. Each card works once a turn (Nudge twice). Unused tiles are lost when you end your turn.

Enchanted tiles turn up now and then, and keep their enchantment when you Nudge or Flip them: 🔥 Flaming adds 4 damage when it attacks, 🛡 Steel fits Vowel Ward even as a consonant and adds 6 block, ✨ Gilded is worth double, and 🌿 Leaf heals you 3 when it's used.

Watch what the foe will do next: attacks, and tricks like Jumble (one of your next tiles changes), Nibble (one fewer tile) and Muffle (vowels worth nothing).
