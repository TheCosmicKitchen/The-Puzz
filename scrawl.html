Dungeon Scrawl

You
How to play

Beat today's three foes. Everyone gets the same foes, the same tiles and the same tricks on offer.

Win the daily and you can keep descending. The same run carries on, kit and all: every third foe is The Editor, foes hit 10% harder every three floors, and the Armoury offers a new trick between every fight. How deep can you go? Your daily result is already counted, and the descent saves as you play.

Each turn you draw six letter tiles. Tap a tile, then a card, or drag the tile onto the card. While you hold a tile, every card shows what it would do with it. Changed tiles come back to your hand, so you can keep working on them. Misplaced one? Undo takes back your last trick, Quill or Strike this turn. Unused tiles are lost when you end your turn.

Spellblade is your main attack: a real word of 3 to 6 letters deals its points, plus a bonus for long words. Use it as often as your tiles allow. Some blade slots are bonus squares: DL doubles the letter that lands there, TL triples it, and DW doubles the whole word if your word reaches it. Each day it has an edge, a bonus for words that fit today's rule: rhymes, palindromes, five-letter words, double letters and so on.

Freeplay (top right) starts a random three-foe crawl with the newest rules straight away. It saves on its own and never touches your daily or your streak.

From the 27 Nov daily (and in Freeplay now) the Spellblade is a sword. Put a tile in any slot, so you can plan a word around it; the word is one unbroken row. The hilt (first slot) gives you block for every letter of a word that starts on it, the crossguard doubles its letter and the tip doubles the word. The Armoury also offers a rune for the sword: it takes no room in your kit, and the same rune again makes it stronger. Up to three runes, three levels each. Tiles you don't use go in your bag (it holds two, enchanted tiles first) and come back next turn, safe from the foe's tricks.

Tricks come in three tiers from the Scrawl Lab's bot trials: common, uncommon and rare. Rare ones turn up less often, and more often the deeper you go. Some let you pick the letter; some take two tiles (tap the one to change, then the one to copy). Some need no tile at all: press the button on the card to draw, roll or pop new letters (the dice are the same for everyone today, and Undo won't reroll them). A socket takes a 3-letter word: drop in three tiles that spell one, then press Set.

Your kit holds four cards: Quill, which flicks a single consonant for its points, and three letter tricks. You start with Nudge (B→C) and Flip (A↔Z) and pick a third before the first foe. After each win you stop at the Armoury: take a new trick in place of one of yours, or keep your kit. Want to try every trick? Open the tool lab.

Enchanted tiles turn up now and then and keep their enchantment through every trick: 🔥 Flaming adds 4 damage, 🛡 Steel gives you 6 block when you use it, ✨ Gilded is worth double, and 🌿 Leaf heals you 3.

Watch what the foe will do next: attacks, and tricks like Jumble (one of your next tiles changes), Nibble (one fewer tile) and Muffle (vowels worth nothing). Every foe has its own trick, said under its name; it lands on your next turn, and the chips above your cards say what's in force.
