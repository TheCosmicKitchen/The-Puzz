How to play
  • Place every piece from the tray. Each piece has a colour and a shape. Together the patches and the clues allow exactly one answer.
  • Patches. The shapes beside the board are every colour patch in the finished box, as they’ll look on the board (no rotating). Squares of the same colour that touch belong to the same patch, so two patches of one colour never touch. Work out where each one fits. Finished patches fade out.
  • Fence. A dashed area holds exactly the shapes on its tag, in any order.
  • Tally. A number at the end of a row or column says how many pieces in that line have that colour or shape. A 0 means none.
  • Links. A round badge compares colour, a diamond badge compares shape. = means the same, ≠ means different.
  • Peek pins one correct piece for you. Your result shows how many peeks you used.
