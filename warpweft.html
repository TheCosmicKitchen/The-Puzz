How to play

Your loom has five threads across (the weft) and five down (the warp). By the end, every row and every column spells a five-letter word.

Where a warp thread crosses on top, its letter is already showing. Where the weft goes over, the square is blank: that's yours to fill. Tap a square and type. Tap it again to switch between across and down.

A row whose word is real pulls tight and gets a knot on the right. A column with a real word gets a gold tassel at the bottom. Any words that fit count, not just ours.

The weave changes through the week, and so does how much you're given: plain weave on Monday and Tuesday, basket weave midweek, herringbone on Friday and twill at the weekend.

Stuck? Tug a thread to reveal a letter. Your result shows how many you tugged.
